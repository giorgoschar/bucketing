11 · Desktop

Same app on a wide screen: the tabs become a sidebar, and lists open a detail pane.

The sidebar has the same places as the phone tabs: Home, Activity, Plan (Bills, Budgets, Cash, Pantry) and Insights. "New expense" stays on screen as a button, and you can press N from anywhere. ⌘K searches everything. On list screens, the item you pick opens in a pane on the right, so the list keeps your scroll position, filters and selection.
