09 · Insights

One period, one lens, everything on the same numbers.

The period chips drive every chart on the screen, and a Household / Me / Maria switch replaces the separate “My money” page. Widgets come in a fixed order, from this month's total down to habits, so there's no per-device settings panel to manage.

10 · Settings

A short hub, with one screen per job.

The 500-line settings page is split up. You open settings from your avatar on Home, see a list of nine rows, and each row opens a focused screen. Category editing and learned rules, which weren't on the web before, now sit together.
