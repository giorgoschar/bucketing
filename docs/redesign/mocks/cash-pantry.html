07 · Plan › Cash

Cash counts as spent the moment you take it out.

Each person gets a wallet per month that the household can see, and an optional stash at home that only its owner can see. The screen does one sum: what you took, minus what you logged, equals what is still unaccounted for. Taking, putting back and counting what's left all happen in one sheet.

08 · Plan › Pantry

Know what's at home, and where it's cheapest today.

Stock is a list of counters you tap up and down, with a minimum for each item, so running low is obvious. Prices come from the PosoKanei price observatory, found by name or barcode. The shopping list groups items by the store where each one is cheapest, and marking items bought adds them to stock and can log the expense.
