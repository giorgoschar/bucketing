05 · Plan › Bills

Bills read as a timeline of what's due. Paying one takes a single sheet.

The old bills page was a table of templates with a modal for every action. Bills now sort by what needs you, then by date, so the next 30 days read like a calendar. Paying, skipping and entering a variable amount each happen in one sheet, and a new bill asks its questions in four short steps.

06 · Plan › Budgets

A budget is a place money goes. Each type shows what that type needs.

Buckets were one generic page with optional cards bolted on. Now a spending budget shows its pace through the month, a trip shows its days and daily spend, and a savings budget shows its goal and the monthly amount needed to reach it on time. Creating one starts by picking the type, and the form adapts.
