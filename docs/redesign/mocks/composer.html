04 · Add & Scan

One composer. A €3 coffee takes two taps.

Type the amount and tap Save. Budget, category, payer and payment method are already filled from your last entry and your merchant rules, shown as pills you can tap to change. This one screen replaces the 4-step expense wizard, the long edit form, the separate income page, the scan confirmation form and the cash "take and spend" form. Everything else waits under "More".
